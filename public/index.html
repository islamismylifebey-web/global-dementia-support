IMMEDIATE DANGER?

Get emergency help first

If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now. This directory is not an emergency dispatch service and does not monitor requests.

If it is safe and useful, you can request your device location and choose to share a map link with a trusted person or responder. Your location is not tracked in the background or sent to this directory.

OUR COMMITMENT

Technology with boundaries. Help with integrity.

AI may help organize and improve directory information. It does not replace professional judgment or verification.

01

No invented listings

Names, contact details, services, and credentials must come from evidence—not guesses.

02

No false verification

Only records checked against reliable sources can carry a verified status.

03

Privacy by design

Sensitive information and precise location are protected and shared only with appropriate permission.

04

No unauthorized dispatch

Professionals must separately opt in before they can be considered for an emergency network.
